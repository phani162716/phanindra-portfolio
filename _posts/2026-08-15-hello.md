I forked [Minimal Mistakes](https://mmistakes.github.io/minimal-mistakes/) and turned it into a 3D portfolio. This is the first note on the result.

I am a **B.Tech ECE graduate** from **SRM University-AP**, now doing **M.Tech at the same college**. I wanted a site that says that in one glance — not a theme preview, not a resume PDF in a browser.

## Why this exists

Most of my work lives in repos. That is fine until I need to explain a decision six months later and the only artifact is a commit message I wrote at 1am.

This site is for the longer version.

## What I will write about

- **The University ERP.** Roles, documents, QR verification, the boring auth work that makes the rest possible.
- **Implementation notes.** Schema choices, things that broke, things I would not do twice.
- **ECE / M.Tech.** FPGA, Vivado, and whatever the postgraduate work turns into when it is finished enough to describe.
